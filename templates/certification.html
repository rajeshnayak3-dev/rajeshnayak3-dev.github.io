{% if live %}How the exam works{% else %}How the exam will work{% endif %}

Questions are drawn at random from a bank of {{ bank_size }} questions across every REVEX section: the RCM cycle, insurance and payers, forms and billing, denials and remark codes, non-denials, specialties and interview topics. Each attempt gets a different set.

  • One answer per question. Unanswered questions are marked wrong. You can flag questions and come back.
  • The timer keeps running if you switch tabs. When it reaches zero, your answers are submitted.
  • Answers are scored by the REVEX exam service (a Google Apps Script run by the site author), not in your browser.
  • If you don't pass, you can retake the exam after {{ cert.retake_hours }} hours.

What the certificate means, and what it doesn't

  • It's a REVEX-issued educational certificate. It shows that the named person passed REVEX's online exam.
  • The exam is open-book and unproctored. Every answer is taught in the lessons, and the answer key is published with the site's source code, so the certificate doesn't prove supervised testing or identity.
  • It is not an accredited, licensing or coding credential, and it isn't endorsed by CMS, X12, AAPC, AHIMA, HFMA, any payer or any employer.

Your data

To issue and verify a certificate, the exam service stores the name you enter, your score, the date and time, and the certificate ID in a Google Sheet owned by the site author. Failed attempts are stored too (name, score, time). The verification page shows only the name, certificate title, issue date and status for an ID someone enters; it can't list or search certificates. To have your record removed, message the author on LinkedIn. Don't enter anything other than your name.

Prepare first: take the quizzes at the end of each lesson and the practice cases. Your progress page shows what's left.
