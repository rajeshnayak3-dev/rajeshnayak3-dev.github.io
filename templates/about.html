About REVEX

REVEX is a free, independent learning and practice site for US healthcare revenue cycle management: for people starting out, associates building judgement, and QA specialists, leads and analysts.

Who writes it

REVEX is written and maintained by {{ site.author.name }}, an {{ site.author.title }} with {{ site.author.experience }}. The lessons are written the way work is actually done on an RCM floor: what you check, what you ask the payer, what you document and what an auditor looks for.

How to use it

  • New to RCM: follow the New to RCM path in order and take every quiz.
  • Working in RCM: follow Working in RCM, use the reference library, and test your judgement in the practice lab.
  • QA, leads and analysts: the QA, senior, team lead and analyst path covers audits, calibration, root cause, CAPA and KPIs.

How content is sourced

Policy-sensitive statements (Medicare rules, ABNs, QMB, filing limits, code-set maintenance) are checked against primary sources such as CMS manuals and regulations, Medicare contractor pages and X12 code lists, and the lesson shows the date of that review. Lessons that haven't been re-reviewed yet say so. The full list is on the official sources page. KPI definitions and workflow advice are common industry practice, not regulation, and are labelled that way.

Privacy and your data

  • No accounts, no tracking scripts. REVEX doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the exam and verification pages load two open-source libraries from cdnjs; those providers receive the usual technical request data (such as your IP address) when your browser fetches them.
  • Learning progress stays in your browser. REVEX stores these items in your browser's local storage, on this device only: lessons marked complete and quiz scores (revex-progress-v1), your colour theme (revex-theme), the last page you read (revex-last), your role self-check (revex-competency-v1) and, if you take the exam, the exam in progress, retake timer and your certificate details (revex-exam-session, revex-exam-lock, revex-cert). Nothing here is sent to REVEX. Clearing your browser data, or the button on Your learning, removes it. It doesn't sync between devices.
  • The exam (only when it's open). To score the exam and issue certificates, the name you enter, your answers' score, the time and the certificate ID are sent to a Google Apps Script service and stored in a Google Sheet owned by the site author. Failed attempts are stored too. The verification page shows only the name, certificate title, issue date and status for an ID someone already has. To have your record removed, contact the author.
  • Calculators and the AR note generator run entirely in your browser; nothing you type is sent or stored.
  • Never enter real patient information (names, dates of birth, member IDs, account numbers or any other PHI) into REVEX, its tools, or any public AI tool.
