About REVEX

REVEX is a free training site for anyone who wants to work in US healthcare revenue cycle management, and for analysts preparing for their next interview.

{{ site.mission }}

Who writes it

REVEX is written and maintained by {{ site.author.name }}, an {{ site.author.title }} with {{ site.author.experience }}. The lessons are written the way work is actually done on an RCM floor: what you check, what you ask the payer, what you document and what an auditor looks for.

How to use it

  • New to RCM: follow the learning path on the home page in order, and take every quiz.
  • Already working: use the denial code library and specialty guides as a reference, and test yourself with the scenarios.
  • Preparing for interviews: read the interview prep section, then explain each RCM stage out loud without notes.

Disclaimers

  • Content is educational. It isn't legal, compliance, coding-certification or payer-specific advice. Payer rules, timely filing limits and code sets change; always confirm against the payer's provider manual, the contract, CMS manuals and the current CPT, HCPCS and ICD-10-CM books.
  • CPT® is a registered trademark of the American Medical Association. Code descriptions here are short, simplified summaries written for learning, not official descriptors.
  • Every patient, member ID, claim number, provider and dollar amount on this site is fictional. No protected health information (PHI) is used.
  • REVEX isn't affiliated with CMS, any payer, clearinghouse or employer.
  • Progress and quiz scores are stored only in your browser. REVEX has no accounts. The only personal data REVEX keeps is from the certification exam: your name, score and certificate ID are stored so certificates can be verified. To have yours removed, message the author on LinkedIn.
