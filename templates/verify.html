Verify a REVEX certificate

Enter the certificate ID printed on the certificate (for example RVX-202610-ABCD2345) to confirm it's genuine.

REVEX certificates confirm that the holder passed the REVEX certification exam in US healthcare revenue cycle management. REVEX is a free, independent training site. Its certificates are educational: the exam is open-book and unproctored, and the certificate isn't an accredited, licensing or coding credential. Verification confirms only that the ID was issued by REVEX to the name shown and hasn't been revoked.
