Official sources and review policy

Where REVEX checks policy-sensitive statements, what each source covers, and when it was last reviewed.

Review policy

  • Statements about Medicare and Medicaid rules, beneficiary protections, filing limits, notices and code-set maintenance are checked against primary sources: CMS manuals and regulations, Medicare contractor (MAC) pages, HHS, and X12.
  • Lessons show when their policy-sensitive statements were last reviewed. Lessons not yet re-reviewed say so.
  • A MAC's page applies to its own jurisdiction. REVEX uses one MAC as an example when rules are similar; check your own MAC.
  • Commercial payer rules come from each payer's provider manual and the provider's contract. REVEX doesn't publish payer-specific rules unless it can link a source.
  • Industry practice (KPI definitions, audit methods, workflow advice) is labelled as practice, not regulation.
  • Found something out of date? Report it on GitHub with the source.
