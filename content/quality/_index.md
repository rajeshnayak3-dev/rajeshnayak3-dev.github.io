---
title: Quality and analytics
description: For QA specialists, senior associates, team leads and analysts. How RCM audits are scoped, sampled, scored and calibrated, how to find root causes, run corrective and preventive action, and prioritize AR with data.
---

These lessons cover the work that sits above individual claims: checking that the work is right, finding out why it isn't, and fixing the process so the same error doesn't come back. The methods are standard quality practice applied to RCM. Your client's or employer's audit standards, scoring rules and SOPs always take priority over the examples here.
