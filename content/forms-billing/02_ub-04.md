## What the UB-04 is

The **UB-04 (Uniform Bill, also called CMS-1450)** is the claim form for **institutional** (facility) charges: hospitals (inpatient and outpatient), skilled nursing facilities, home health agencies, hospices, rehab facilities, dialysis centers and some others. Its electronic version is the **837I**.

Fields on the UB-04 are called **form locators (FLs)**, numbered 1 to 81.

## Type of bill (FL 4)

A four-digit code, with a leading **0**, then three meaningful digits.

**Digit 2: type of facility**

| Digit | Facility |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hospital |
| 2 | Skilled nursing facility |
| 3 | Home health |
| 7 | Clinic (for example, rural health clinic, FQHC, ESRD facility) |
| 8 | Specialty facility (for example, hospice, critical access hospital) |

**Digit 3: bill classification** (for hospitals and SNFs)

| Digit | Classification |
|---|---|
| 1 | Inpatient (including Part A) |
| 2 | Inpatient (Part B only) |
| 3 | Outpatient |
| 4 | Other (Part B) |

**Digit 4: frequency**

| Digit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Admit through discharge claim |
| 2 | Interim, first claim |
| 3 | Interim, continuing claim |
| 4 | Interim, last claim |
| 7 | **Replacement** of prior claim |
| 8 | **Void or cancel** prior claim |

Common TOBs: **0111** inpatient hospital, complete stay; **0131** hospital outpatient; **0137** outpatient replacement; **0211** SNF inpatient; **0321** home health; **0811** hospice; **0851** critical access hospital outpatient.

## Revenue codes (FL 42)

Revenue codes tell the payer **which department or type of charge** each line is. Some must be paired with a HCPCS code on outpatient claims.

|---|---|
| 0110–0119 | Room and board, private |
| 0120–0129 | Room and board, semi-private |
| 0200–0209 | Intensive care |
| 0250 | Pharmacy, general |
| 0270 / 0272 / 0278 | Medical and surgical supplies; sterile supplies; other implants |
| 0300–0319 | Laboratory and pathology |
| 0320–0329 | Radiology, diagnostic |
| 0350–0359 | CT scan |
| 0360 | Operating room services |
| 0370 | Anesthesia |
| 0420 / 0430 / 0440 | Physical, occupational, speech therapy |
| 0450 | Emergency room |
| 0481 / 0730 | Cardiac catheterization lab; EKG/ECG |

| 0610 | MRI |
| 0636 | Drugs requiring detailed coding (with HCPCS and NDC) |

| 0001 | Total charges line |

A revenue code that doesn't fit the HCPCS code on the same line leads to **CO-199** (revenue code and procedure code do not match).

## Condition, occurrence and value codes

These small codes change how a claim is processed. Examples:

- **Condition codes (FL 18–28):** 44 (inpatient changed to outpatient by utilization review), 20 (beneficiary requested billing for a denial notice), D0–E0 range (changes on replacement claims, such as D1 changes to charges).
- **Occurrence codes (FL 31–34):** 01 auto accident, 02 no-fault insurance involved, 04 accident at work, 11 onset of symptoms, 24 date insurance denied.
- **Value codes (FL 39–41):** 80 covered days, 81 non-covered days, 06 Medicare blood deductible, 12–16 MSP-related amounts (for example 12 working aged).

## The present on admission (POA) indicator

On inpatient claims, each diagnosis carries a **POA indicator**:

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Y | Present at the time of admission |
| N | Not present at admission (developed in hospital) |
| U | Documentation insufficient to determine |
| W | Clinically undetermined |
| 1 (or blank) | Exempt from POA reporting |

For certain **hospital-acquired conditions (HACs)**, Medicare won't pay the higher DRG if the condition wasn't present on admission.

!!! tip "Professional versus facility, same visit"
    An emergency visit creates two claims: the hospital bills its ER facility charge (revenue code 0450 with an ER visit level such as 99284 in FL 44) on a UB-04, and the ER physician bills 99284 on a CMS-1500 with POS 23. See [hospital versus professional billing](/forms-billing/hospital-vs-professional-billing/).

!!! mistake "Auditor's red flag"
    Replacement claims (TOB xx7) sent without the original document control number in FL 64. The payer treats them as new claims and denies them as duplicates.

!!! interview "If an interviewer asks “What is a type of bill?”"
    “The type of bill is the four-digit code in FL 4 of the UB-04. After the leading zero, the second digit is the facility type, the third is the bill classification such as inpatient or outpatient, and the fourth is the frequency, like 1 for admit through discharge, 7 for a replacement claim and 8 for a void. For example, 0131 is a hospital outpatient claim.”
