---
title: Non-denials
description: Unpaid claims that weren't denied. Rejections, claims with no response, underpayments, paid-but-not-received claims, secondary and crossover failures, credit balances, refunds and recoupments.
---

Not every problem claim carries a denial code. Many sit in AR because they never reached the payer, are stuck in process, were paid to the wrong place, or were paid the wrong amount. These are often harder to spot than denials, because nothing on an ERA points to them.
