## How to practise

Read each call with a friend playing the representative. Then close the page and do it again from memory, changing the details. Interviewers listen for four things: a clear opening with provider details, the right questions in a sensible order, polite control of the call, and a reference number at the end.

All names, IDs and numbers below are fictional.

## Call 1: claim not on file

!!! example "Call"
    **Rep:** Thank you for calling. Who am I speaking with?

    **You:** Hi, this is Arjun calling on behalf of Lakeview Family Medicine. I'm calling about claim status. The provider NPI is 1234567893 and the tax ID is 98-7654321.

    **Rep:** Patient details, please.

    **You:** Patient name Laura Kim, date of birth 07/22/1985, member ID QAB123456789. Date of service 08/12/2026, billed amount $165.

    **Rep:** I don't see a claim on file for that date.

    **You:** Could you also search by the subscriber's ID and a date range from 08/01 to 08/31, in case it was received under a different date?

    **Rep:** Still nothing.

    **You:** Thank you. Can you confirm the correct payer ID and claims address for electronic and paper claims?

    **Rep:** Payer ID 12345 electronically, or PO Box 100, Anytown.

    **You:** And the timely filing limit for this plan?

    **Rep:** 90 days from the date of service.

    **You:** Great. We'll resubmit today. May I have your name and a call reference number?

    **Rep:** Maria, reference 4471-2290.

**Note:** “10/14/26 Called payer, spoke to Maria, ref 4471-2290. Claim for DOS 08/12 not on file (searched by member and subscriber ID, 08/01–08/31). Payer ID 12345 confirmed. TFL 90 days from DOS (11/10). Clearinghouse shows 08/15 rejection for invalid member ID; corrected ID per card and resubmitted electronically 10/14. F/U 10/28.”

## Call 2: denial for missing authorization

!!! example "Call"
    **You:** ...date of service 09/03/2026, CPT 70553, billed $1,850. Can you tell me the claim status?

    **Rep:** It was denied on 09/20 because no authorization was on file.

    **You:** Thank you. Can you check whether an authorization exists for this member under any provider for 70553 around that date?

    **Rep:** I see an authorization for 70551, approved 08/28 through 09/27, for your provider.

    **You:** That's helpful. The radiologist added contrast, so 70553 was performed. Does the plan allow an authorization update after the service, and how?

    **Rep:** Updates are accepted within 14 days of the service through the portal. That window has passed, so you'd need to appeal.

    **You:** Understood. What's the appeal time limit and where should we send it?

    **Rep:** 180 days from the denial date, through the portal or fax 555-0123.

    **You:** Thank you. May I have your name and reference number?

**Note:** “10/14/26 Called payer, spoke to Dev, ref 8820-1144. DOS 09/03, 70553 denied 09/20 CO-197. Auth on file for 70551 (08/28–09/27); protocol changed to with/without contrast. Auth update window (14 days) passed. Appeal limit 180 days from denial (03/19/27), portal or fax 555-0123. Requested radiology report and order from client to appeal for medical necessity of contrast. F/U 10/21.”

## Call 3: paid, but payment not found

!!! example "Call"
    **Rep:** The claim was paid on 09/02 by check, $96.40.

    **You:** Could you give me the check number, the total check amount and the address it was mailed to?

    **Rep:** Check 0045521, total $1,212.80, mailed to 200 Main Street, Suite 4.

    **You:** Thank you. Our current address is 18 Park Avenue, so it went to our old address. Has the check been cashed?

    **Rep:** No, it's still outstanding.

    **You:** Can you place a stop payment and reissue it to our current address? What do you need from us?

    **Rep:** We'll need an updated W-9 with the new address, faxed to 555-0199. Reissue takes 10 to 15 business days after that.

    **You:** We'll fax it today. May I have your name and reference number?

**Note:** “10/14/26 Called payer, spoke to Leah, ref 3301-5560. Claim paid 09/02, $96.40, on check 0045521 (bulk $1,212.80) mailed to old address (200 Main St). Check not cashed. Requested stop payment and reissue; updated W-9 to be faxed to 555-0199. Reissue 10–15 business days after W-9. Sent W-9 request to client. F/U 11/04.”

## Call 4: other insurance on file

!!! example "Call"
    **Rep:** The claim was denied because the member has other coverage that's primary.

    **You:** Could you tell me the other insurance on file, with its effective dates?

    **Rep:** An Aetna employer plan from 01/01/2024, no end date.

    **You:** Our records show the patient's Aetna coverage ended in June. How can the coordination of benefits record be updated?

    **Rep:** The member needs to call us and complete a COB update, or send the termination letter from Aetna.

    **You:** Once that's done, will the claim be reprocessed automatically, or should we call back?

    **Rep:** Call back after the update to request reprocessing.

**Note:** “10/14/26 Called payer, spoke to Ken, ref 7719-0032. Denied CO-22: Aetna on file as primary since 01/01/24, no term date. Per registration and patient, Aetna termed 06/30/26. Member must update COB by phone or send Aetna term letter. Contacted patient 10/14, left message with payer's member services number. Will request reprocessing after update. F/U 10/24.”

## Voicemail script

When a representative or patient doesn't answer:

> “Hello, this is [your name] calling on behalf of [provider name] about a claim for [patient's first name only, or ‘a patient’] with a date of service of [date]. Please call us back at [number], extension [x], between [hours]. Thank you.”

Leave the minimum details. Don't leave diagnoses, balances or full patient names on a voicemail (HIPAA minimum necessary).

!!! tip "What interviewers listen for"
    You don't need to sound American. You need to be clear, polite, structured and confident with numbers. Read dates as “oh-eight, twelve, twenty twenty-six” or “August twelfth”, and spell IDs using a phonetic alphabet (“Q as in Queen, A as in Apple”).
