  - q: What is CO-18?
    options:
      - Duplicate claim
      - Timely filing
      - Authorization absent
      - Deductible
    answer: 0
    why: CARC 18 is an exact duplicate claim or service.
  - q: Which box on the CMS-1500 holds the diagnosis pointer?
    options:
      - 21
      - 24E
      - 24J
      - "33"
    answer: 1
    why: 24E points each line to diagnoses in box 21.
  - q: What type of bill is a hospital outpatient claim?
    options:
      - "0111"
      - "0131"
      - "0211"
      - "0811"
    answer: 1
    why: 013x is hospital outpatient.
  - q: Modifier 26 means what?
    options:
      - Technical component
      - Professional component
      - Bilateral
      - Distinct service
    answer: 1
    why: 26 is the professional (interpretation) component.
  - q: What is the Medicare timely filing limit?
    options:
      - 90 days
      - 6 months
      - 12 months from the date of service
      - 2 years
    answer: 2
    why: Original Medicare claims must be filed within one calendar year of the date of service.
