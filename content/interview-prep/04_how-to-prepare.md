## Practise a mock AR call

Most AR roles include a voice test where you play an AR caller. Practise this script with a friend until it sounds natural.

!!! example "Mock call (fictional)"
    **Representative:** Thank you for calling. How can I help you?

    **You:** Hi, this is Priya calling from ABC Medical Group on behalf of Dr Lee. I'm calling to check the status of a claim. The provider's NPI is 1234567890 and the tax ID is 12-3456789.

    **Rep:** Can I have the patient's name, date of birth and member ID?

    **You:** Sure. The patient is John Carter, date of birth 03/15/1972, member ID XYZ123456789. The date of service is 08/04/2026 and the billed amount is $245.

    **Rep:** I see the claim. It was denied on 08/28 for no authorization.

    **You:** Thank you. Can you confirm the denial code, and whether there's any authorization on file for this member for CPT 72148 around that date?

    **Rep:** The code is CO-197. I don't see an authorization.

    **You:** Does the plan allow a retro-authorization for this service, and within what time frame?

    **Rep:** Retro requests are accepted within 30 days of the date of service through the portal.

    **You:** Understood. If the retro-auth isn't approved, what's the appeal time limit and where should it be sent?

    **Rep:** 180 days from the denial, by the portal or fax 555-0100.

    **You:** Thank you. May I have your name and a call reference number?

    **Rep:** Anna, reference 2026-1014-778.

    **You:** Thank you, Anna. Have a great day.

Then write the note:

> 10/14/26 Called payer, spoke to Anna, ref 2026-1014-778. DOS 08/04 72148 denied 08/28 CO-197, no auth on file. Retro-auth allowed within 30 days of DOS via portal (window passed). Appeal limit 180 days from denial, portal or fax 555-0100. Sent to client to confirm whether auth was obtained under another provider; if not, prepare appeal with records. F/U 10/21.

## Talk about experience with real examples

Use the **STAR** structure: situation, task, action, result.

!!! tip "Example answer"
    **Situation:** “In my AR project, one Medicare Advantage plan was denying many echocardiograms with CO-197.”

    **Task:** “I had to work the denials and find out why they started.”

    **Action:** “I pulled a report and saw they all started in July. On a call, the payer confirmed it had added echo to its authorization list. I told my lead and the client's scheduling team, and requested retro-authorizations where allowed.”

    **Result:** “We recovered most of the claims, and new echo claims stopped denying once the scheduling team added the auth check.”

## On your CV

- List **payers, specialties and systems** you've worked with (for example Medicare, BCBS, cardiology, Epic, eClinicalWorks, Availity).
- Quantify where you can: claims worked per day, quality score, AR reduction, denial overturn rate.
- For freshers: list RCM knowledge areas you've studied and any certificates (and that you completed REVEX if you did).

## On the day

- Speak slowly and clearly, especially in voice rounds.
- When you don't know an answer, say how you'd find out (payer portal, provider manual, your lead). That's what the job requires anyway.
- Ask one good question at the end, such as “Which payers and specialties would I work on first?”
