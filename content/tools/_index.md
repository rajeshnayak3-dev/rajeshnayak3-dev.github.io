---
title: AR tools
description: Calculators and generators for daily AR work. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent or saved anywhere.
---

These tools do the arithmetic and formatting so you can focus on the claim. They follow common industry rules, but payer contracts, program rules and client SOPs always take priority. Each tool shows its assumptions; check the result against the payer's own rules before you act on it.

Use fictional or de-identified data only. Don't enter real patient details (PHI) into any public tool.
